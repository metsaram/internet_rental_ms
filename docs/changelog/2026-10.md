# Changelog — October 2026

## [2026-10-30]
### Added
- **Unified Service Import with Schedule & Billing** — Moved "Schedule & Duration" (`startDate`, `durationMonths`, `freeMonths`) and "Financial Terms & Billing" (`monthlyFee`, `currency`, `billingCycle`) directly into `SERVICE_FIELDS` and `import.tsx`. Users who don't need contracts can import services with their full billing terms in a single Excel sheet.
- **Direct Invoice Linkage to Services (Contract-Free Mode)** — Invoices now support linking directly to `InternetService` (`serviceId`). When `Company.useContract = false`, users can create invoices by selecting an installed service directly, automatically pulling monthly fee, currency, and attributing spend to branches and shops.
- **Sectioned Import Expected Fields** — Reorganized the Expected Fields card in `import.tsx` into 3 clean groups:
  1. Service & Site Identification
  2. Schedule & Duration
  3. Financial Terms & Billing
- **Extended Service Excel Template** — Updated `/api/template/service` to include columns for Start Date, Duration Months, Free Months, Monthly Fee, Currency, and Billing Cycle.
- **Contract-Free Dashboard Aggregation** — Dashboard KPI cards and charts aggregate active monthly spend by currency, provider, and branch directly from `internet_services` when operating in contract-free mode.
- **Settings: "Require contract for invoices" toggle** — New boolean company setting `useContract` (default `true`). When disabled, invoices can be created without linking a contract. Toggle visible in Settings → Company profile (admin only). Changes persist via `PATCH /api/company`.
- **Invoice: Contract-Matching Schedule & Finance Structure** — Replaced the quick-fill box in `InvoiceForm` with the exact two-card structure from `ContractForm`:
  - `2. Schedule & Duration`: Start date, Duration (months), Free months (Zero-fee extension), and End date (auto-calculated, extended by free months).
  - `3. Financial Terms & Billing`: Agreed Monthly fee, Currency, Billing cycle.
  - `4. Live Cost Projection`: Billable Months, Scheduled Total (Subtotal), Effective Monthly, followed by Tax / VAT, Discount, and Total Payable.
- **Contract & Service Pre-fill Parity** — Selecting a contract or service in `InvoiceForm` automatically pre-fills start date, duration, free months, agreed monthly fee, currency, and billing cycle.
- **Fee Calculation Option (Monthly vs Yearly Fee Input)** — Both `InvoiceForm` and `ContractForm` now support toggling fee calculation basis between Monthly fee and Yearly fee:
  - Header pill toggle: `[ Monthly fee ] [ Yearly fee ]`
  - Dropdown `<Select>`: `Fee calculation` ("Monthly fee" / "Yearly fee")
  - Dynamic fee input: dynamically re-labels to `Agreed Monthly fee` or `Agreed Yearly fee`
  - Automatic bidirectional scaling: converts existing fee value on toggle (`* 12` or `/ 12`)
  - Live conversion hint: shows `≈ $60.50/mo` when inputting yearly fee, and `≈ $726.00/yr` when inputting monthly fee
  - Live cost projection, scheduled subtotal, and 10% tax calculate accurately based on derived monthly rate and duration.
- **Service Installation Fee Option with Input Amount** — Added a dedicated option checkbox and amount field in both `InvoiceForm` and `ContractForm`:
  - Checkbox: `[ ] Service installation fee` with `<Wrench />` icon.
  - Amount input: disabled with muted styling when unchecked (`0.00`), immediately enabled when checked.
  - Combined subtotal: `subtotal = scheduled + installationFee`. 10% Tax is computed on the total taxable subtotal.
  - Itemized Total Breakdown: shows Scheduled Service, Service Installation Fee (+amount), Subtotal (Price Excluded), Tax (10%), Discount, and Total Payable.
  - Detail Sheet Parity: `InvoiceDetailSheet` and `ContractDetailSheet` display itemized installation fee line item.
  - Persistence & Pre-fill: preserved across edits via audit tag and auto-populated when selecting a contract or service.
- **DatePicker Month and Year Selectors (5-Year Default Range)** — Enhanced `DatePicker` and `DateRangePicker` in `src/components/ui/date-picker.tsx` with native Month and Year dropdown selectors (`captionLayout="dropdown"`), defaulting to a 5-year range (`currentYear - 5` to `currentYear + 5`). Automatically expands range when displaying dates outside the 5-year span. Supports custom `yearRange`, `startMonth`, `endMonth`, and fallback `captionLayout="label"`.

### Changed
- `InternetService` model: added `startDate`, `endDate`, `durationMonths`, `freeMonths`, `monthlyFee`, `currency`, `billingCycle` columns (`db/2026-10-30/services/add_service_schedule_and_billing.sql`).
- `Invoice` model: made `contractId` nullable (`String?`) and added `serviceId` (`String?`) relation to `InternetService`.
- `Company` model: added `useContract Boolean @default(true)` column (`db/2026-10-30/companies/add_use_contract.sql`).
- `/api/invoices` GET & POST: accepts `serviceId`, includes both `contract` and `service`, and resolves branch, shop, and provider safely whether linked via contract or directly via service.
- `InvoiceDetailSheet`: gracefully displays service, branch, shop, and provider without crashing on contract-free invoices.

### Removed
- **Billing Cycle Dropdown & Badges** — Removed legacy "Billing cycle" selector (Yearly, Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-annual) from `InvoiceForm`, `ContractForm`, and `RenewDialog` because all billing schedules and cost projections are strictly derived from Schedule & Duration (`startDate`, `durationMonths`, `freeMonths`, `endDate`). Rebalanced Section 3 into a clean 3-column layout (`Fee calculation`, `Agreed fee`, `Currency`) and replaced cycle badges with dynamic schedule period badges (`startDate → endDate`).

### Tests

- `test/invoice-schedule.test.tsx`: 33 tests covering contract-matching timeline calculation (free months extension), scheduled subtotal, auto-calculated 10% tax (price excluded), custom rate/amount overrides, installation fees, and schedule-based billing without billing cycle.
